How to calculate VAT in South Africa

South Africa's standard VAT rate is 15%, set by SARS (South African Revenue Service). Most goods and services are subject to VAT, with some zero-rated exceptions like basic foodstuffs and exports.

To add VAT to an amount (price excluding VAT):

VAT Amount = Price × 0.15  |  Total = Price × 1.15

To remove VAT from an amount (price including VAT):

Original Price = Total ÷ 1.15  |  VAT Amount = Total − Original Price
