How bond repayments are calculated

A South African home loan (bond) uses a standard amortisation formula. Each monthly payment covers both interest and a portion of the outstanding principal, so the balance reduces to zero over the loan term.

M = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n − 1]
Where: P = loan amount  |  r = monthly rate (annual ÷ 12)  |  n = months (years × 12)

The SA Prime Rate is currently 11.25% (as of May 2025). Most major banks — Absa, FNB, Nedbank, Standard Bank and SA Home Loans — offer rates between prime and prime + 2%, depending on your credit profile and deposit size. A 10% deposit typically attracts a better rate than a 0% deposit loan.

The default rate used here is prime + 0.5% (11.75%), a common starting point for borrowers with good credit. Adjust the rate field to match your bank's offer.

Total interest paid is shown in red to illustrate the long-term cost of the loan. A larger deposit or a shorter term significantly reduces total interest. Note that this calculator does not include bond registration costs, transfer duties or monthly bank fees.
