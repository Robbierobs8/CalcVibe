How retrenchment severance works in South Africa

Under Labour Relations Act (LRA) Section 41, if you are retrenched for operational requirements, your employer must pay you a minimum of one week's remuneration for every completed year of continuous service.

Severance Pay = Completed Years of Service × (Monthly Salary × 12 ÷ 52)

Key things to know:

  • This is the legal minimum — many employers offer more as part of a negotiated package.
  • Operational requirement (Section 189) retrenchments qualify. Misconduct or poor performance dismissals do not.
  • If you have less than one completed year of service, no minimum severance applies — but you are still entitled to notice pay.
  • Notice pay is calculated separately and is in addition to severance.
  • The total package may be partially tax-exempt under retirement lump sum tax tables — consult a tax professional for your specific situation.

Your retrenchment package may also include: pro-rata bonus, leave payout, and any unpaid salary owed to you.
